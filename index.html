The cortisol patch: stress hormones from sweat

A thin, round patch that briefly induces sweat, then measures three stress hormones in five snapshots about six minutes apart. Cortisol tracks longer-running stress; epinephrine and norepinephrine track the fast fight-or-flight response.

Inside the patch

3 hormonesCortisol, epinephrine, norepinephrine
5 snapshotsAbout 6 minutes apart, each in its own chamber
pg/mlDetects epinephrine and norepinephrine at picogram levels
WirelessBluetooth to the phone; pH, salt and temperature correct the readings
Research status. This patch follows the Stressomic design from Caltech (Tu et al., Science Advances, 2025), shown in pilot human studies. In that design, one patch captures a single session of about an hour. Making it last 21 days, as Leeway’s Pro plan assumes, would need further development: reagents are used up and sweat is induced on demand.
